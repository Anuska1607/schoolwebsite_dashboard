School Information

🏫 About Our School

Founded in 1995, our school is committed to academic excellence, holistic development, and community engagement. We offer a nurturing environment where students thrive intellectually and socially.

🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision: To inspire lifelong learning and responsible citizenship.
Mission: To deliver inclusive, innovative, and student-centered education.

👩‍🏫 Faculty & Staff

Our faculty includes experienced educators, counselors, and support staff who work collaboratively to ensure every student’s success.

🏢 Facilities

  • Smart classrooms
  • Science and computer labs
  • Library with digital access
  • Sports and recreation areas
  • Art, music, and drama studios

📍 Location

We are located in Rajarhat, West Bengal, India. Easily accessible via public transport and surrounded by a peaceful neighborhood.

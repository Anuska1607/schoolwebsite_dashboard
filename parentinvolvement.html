🤝 Parent Involvement

We believe that strong parent-school partnerships lead to better learning outcomes and a more vibrant school community. Here's how you can get involved:

📚 Volunteer in Classrooms

Support teachers during reading time, art projects, or science experiments.

🎉 Help Organize Events

Join our event planning committee for school fairs, exhibitions, and cultural programs.

🗣 Attend Parent-Teacher Meetings

Stay informed about your child's progress and collaborate with educators.

📝 Join the Parent Advisory Board

Share your ideas and feedback to help shape school policies and initiatives.

💻 Support Digital Learning

Assist with tech workshops or help students access online resources.


Interested in participating? Contact us or speak with your child’s class teacher to learn more.

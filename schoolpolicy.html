School Policy

📚 Academic Integrity

Students are expected to uphold honesty and integrity in all academic work. Cheating, plagiarism, and other forms of academic dishonesty are strictly prohibited.

🎓 Attendance and Punctuality

Regular attendance is mandatory. Students must arrive on time and attend all scheduled classes unless excused for valid reasons.

🧑‍🏫 Code of Conduct

Respect for teachers, staff, and fellow students is essential. Disruptive behavior, bullying, or harassment will not be tolerated.

📵 Use of Technology

Mobile phones and electronic devices must be used responsibly. Unauthorized use during class hours is prohibited.

🎒 Dress Code

Students must adhere to the school’s dress code policy. Uniforms should be neat, clean, and worn as prescribed.

🏫 Campus Safety

Safety is a top priority. Students must follow all safety guidelines and report any concerns to school authorities immediately.
